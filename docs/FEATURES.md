# DramaSnap - Feature Specifications

## Core Features

### 1. Content Discovery

#### Home Feed
- Personalized movie recommendations based on viewing history
- "Continue Watching" section for in-progress movies
- "Trending Now" - Popular movies across the platform
- "New Releases" - Latest AI-generated content
- Genre-specific carousels (Romance, Mystery, Drama)
- "Because You Watched" - Similar content recommendations

#### Search & Explore
- Search by:
  - Genre
  - Mood (heartwarming, suspenseful, emotional, etc.)
  - Duration (short movies: 30-50 min, full movies: 90-120 min)
  - Themes (betrayal, redemption, revenge, love, etc.)
- Advanced filters:
  - Character count
  - Setting (modern, historical, fantasy)
  - Tone (light, dark, comedic)
  - Ending type (happy, sad, open, twist)

#### Smart Collections
- "Quick Break" - 30-minute movies
- "Deep Dive" - 2+ hour epics
- "Feel-Good Friday" - Uplifting content
- "Mystery Monday" - Thriller content
- User-created playlists

### 2. Viewing Experience

#### Video Player
- Vertical video format (9:16 aspect ratio)
- Swipe up for next episode (TikTok-style)
- Swipe down for previous episode
- Tap to pause/play
- Double-tap to like/favorite
- Episode progress indicator at top
- Movie progress (e.g., "Episode 23/100")
- Auto-play next episode (2-second countdown)
- 10-second skip forward/backward
- Playback speed control (0.5x - 2x)
- Quality settings (Auto, 720p, 1080p, 4K)

#### Binge Mode
- Continuous playback across episodes
- "Skip Recap" button (for 3-second recaps)
- Episode markers for key moments
- Haptic feedback for plot twists
- Picture-in-Picture support
- Background audio playback

#### Interactive Elements
- Poll questions during episodes (choose character decisions)
- Reaction emojis that appear on screen
- Live commenting with other viewers
- Share specific moments with timestamp links

### 3. Personalization Engine

#### AI Recommendation System
- Watch history analysis
- Completion rate tracking
- Like/dislike patterns
- Skip behavior analysis
- Time-of-day preferences
- Binge patterns
- Genre mixing preferences
- Character archetype preferences

#### Custom Preferences
- Favorite genres (select 3-5)
- Content intensity (mild, moderate, intense)
- Romance level (low, medium, high)
- Violence/action level
- Humor preference
- Ending preference (happy vs. realistic)
- Pace preference (slow-burn vs. fast-paced)

#### Mood Matching
- "How are you feeling?" prompt on app open
- Mood-based recommendations:
  - Happy: Light romcoms, feel-good stories
  - Sad: Cathartic dramas, uplifting tales
  - Stressed: Escapist fantasy, cozy mysteries
  - Bored: High-intensity thrillers, plot twists
  - Romantic: Love stories, passionate drama
  - Curious: Mystery, psychological thrillers

### 4. Social Features

#### Community
- Follow other users
- See what friends are watching
- Share favorite movies
- Create watch parties (synchronized viewing)
- Comment on movies
- Rate and review (1-5 stars)
- Create and share playlists

#### Achievements & Gamification
- Badges:
  - "Binge Master" - Watch 10 full movies
  - "Night Owl" - Watch after midnight
  - "Genre Explorer" - Watch 5 different genres
  - "Loyal Viewer" - 30-day streak
  - "Early Adopter" - Watch new releases within 24 hours
- Streaks for daily watching
- Leaderboards (friends only)
- Unlock exclusive content through achievements

### 5. Content Creation (Future)

#### AI Co-Creation
- Users can influence story generation:
  - Choose premise/setting
  - Select character types
  - Pick genre blend
  - Set story length
  - Define ending preference
- Save custom story prompts
- Share user-generated story ideas

### 6. Offline Mode (Premium)

- Download full movies or specific episodes
- Smart downloads (auto-download recommended content)
- Storage management
- Download quality selection
- Auto-delete watched content

### 7. Notifications

#### Smart Notifications
- New episode releases for followed series
- "Come back" reminders for unfinished movies (3 days after)
- Daily personalized recommendations
- Friend activity ("Sarah just finished a movie you'd love")
- Trending content alerts
- Weekly content digest

#### Customization
- Notification frequency (high, medium, low, off)
- Quiet hours
- Notification types (select which to receive)

### 8. Subscription Management

#### In-App Features
- Easy plan switching
- Usage statistics (hours watched, movies completed)
- Subscription benefits overview
- Gift subscriptions to friends
- Family plan (up to 5 profiles)
- Student discount (40% off)

#### Retention Features
- "Take a Break" - Pause subscription for 1-3 months
- Win-back offers for cancelled users
- Loyalty rewards (1 free month after 12 months)
- Referral program (1 month free per friend)

### 9. Accessibility

- Closed captions (auto-generated, editable)
- Audio descriptions
- High contrast mode
- Font size adjustment
- Screen reader support
- Color blind friendly UI
- Reduced motion option

### 10. Analytics & Insights

#### Personal Stats (Premium)
- Total watch time
- Favorite genres (pie chart)
- Most-watched actors/characters
- Completion rate
- Average episode rating
- Peak viewing times
- Binge vs. casual viewing ratio
- Year in review summary

### 11. Quality & Performance

- Adaptive bitrate streaming
- Pre-buffering next episodes
- Low-data mode (for limited data plans)
- Offline-first architecture
- <3 second app launch time
- <1 second episode transition time
- Background playback
- Chromecast/AirPlay support

## Feature Prioritization

### MVP (Phase 1)
1. Home feed with recommendations
2. Video player (vertical, swipe navigation)
3. Basic search
4. User accounts & authentication
5. Subscription system
6. Like/favorite functionality
7. Watch history

### Phase 2
1. Advanced personalization
2. Social features (follow, share)
3. Offline downloads
4. Multiple profiles per account
5. Achievements & gamification

### Phase 3
1. Interactive elements (polls, choices)
2. Watch parties
3. AI co-creation tools
4. Advanced analytics
5. Community features
