# DramaSnap - Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Model Overview

DramaSnap operates on a freemium subscription model with three paid tiers, supported by advertising for free users.

### Revenue Streams

1. **Subscriptions** (Primary): 85% of revenue
2. **Advertising** (Secondary): 12% of revenue
3. **Partnerships & Licensing** (Future): 3% of revenue

## Subscription Tiers

### Free Tier

**Price:** $0

**Access:**
- 9 episodes per day (3 ad breaks)
- Standard quality (720p)
- Limited personalization
- Ads between episodes (15-second ads every 3 episodes)

**Purpose:**
- User acquisition
- Product trial
- Data collection for personalization
- Conversion funnel entry point

**Limitations:**
- Daily viewing limit
- Ad interruptions
- No downloads
- Standard quality only
- Basic recommendations

**Expected User Base:** 60% of total users

### Weekly Pass

**Price:** $4.99/week (~$20/month equivalent)

**Access:**
- Unlimited episodes
- Ad-free experience
- HD quality (1080p)

**Purpose:**
- Low commitment trial

**Target Audience:**

- Occasional bingers
- Budget-conscious viewers

**Conversion Strategy:**

## Pricing Psychology

### Price Points Rationale

**Why $12.99/month?**
- Below Netflix ($15.99) but above niche services ($9.99)
- Perceived as premium but affordable
- Charm pricing (below $13 threshold)
- Market research sweet spot for niche content

**Why $4.99/week?**
- Impulse purchase territory (<$5)
- "Less than a coffee" comparison
- Low commitment testing
- Higher ARPU if used long-term (intentional)

**Why $99.99/year?**
- Below $100 psychological threshold
- 35% savings (compelling value)
- ~$8.33/month (below Netflix)
- Annual commitment = stability

## Conversion Funnel

### Stage 1: Awareness → Download

**Channels:**
- App Store optimization (ASO)
- Social media ads (TikTok, Instagram)
- Influencer partnerships
- Word of mouth
- Referral program

**Goal:** 100,000 downloads in Month 1

### Stage 2: Download → Active User

**Definition:** Watch at least 9 episodes (1 day of free tier)

**Tactics:**
- Strong onboarding experience
- Personalization questionnaire
- Immediate content recommendation
- First episode optimized for engagement

**Goal:** 60% activation rate

### Stage 3: Active User → Free Tier Engagement

**Tactics:**
- Daily viewing limit (creates desire for more)
- Strategic ad placement (not too annoying)
- Cliffhangers at episode 9 (daily limit)
- "Continue watching tomorrow" reminder

**Goal:** 40% return next day

### Stage 4: Free → Paid Conversion

**Triggers:**
- Hit daily limit (9 episodes)
- Mid-season cliffhanger
- Friend is premium subscriber
- High engagement (3+ days consecutive)

**Tactics:**
- Offer trial period (7 days free with $4.99/week)
- Show benefits: "Watch 30 more episodes today!"
- Social proof: "Join 50,000+ premium members"
- Urgency: "Limited time: 20% off first month"
- Value framing: "$0.43 per episode vs. $1.99 on Amazon"

**Goal:** 8-12% conversion rate (industry standard: 5-10%)

### Stage 5: Weekly → Monthly Upgrade

**Triggers:**
- After 2-3 weeks on weekly plan
- Show cost comparison ($20/month vs. $12.99/month)
- "You're spending more on weekly. Upgrade and save!"

**Goal:** 60% upgrade rate

### Stage 6: Monthly → Yearly Upgrade

**Triggers:**
- After 3+ months of consistent usage
- High engagement (watching daily)
- Holiday seasons (gift to self)
- Birthday (personalized offer)

**Tactics:**
- Email campaign with savings calculator
- In-app banner after session
- "Biggest fans save big!" messaging
- Limited-time bonus (extra month free)

**Goal:** 20% upgrade rate

## Retention Strategy

### Churn Prevention

**Early Warning Signals:**
- Decreased viewing frequency
- Lower completion rates
- No login for 7+ days
- Subscription renewal upcoming
- Recent poor rating

**Intervention Tactics:**

**Tier 1: Engagement Drop (Day 3-7 inactive)**
- Push notification: "New mysteries you'll love!"
- Personalized email with recommendations
- In-app message on next login

**Tier 2: Serious Risk (Day 7-14 inactive)**
- "We miss you!" campaign
- Exclusive content offer
- Survey: "What would bring you back?"
- Discount offer (20% off next month)

**Tier 3: Cancellation Intent**
- Exit survey (required before cancel)
- Offer pause subscription (1-3 months)
- Downgrade to free tier option
- Win-back offer (50% off for 3 months)
- "One more month free to reconsider"

### Retention Tactics

**Monthly:**
- Personalized viewing report ("You watched 20 hours!")
- New content highlights
- Exclusive subscriber content
- Community engagement features
- Achievement milestones

**Quarterly:**
- Subscriber appreciation events
- Early access to premium features
- Survey for content preferences
- Special offers (bring a friend)

**Yearly:**
- Year in review (personalized stats)
- Loyalty rewards (badges, exclusive content)
- Anniversary gift (free month for 1-year subscribers)
- VIP status (beta features, priority support)

### Target Churn Rates

- Monthly: <8% (industry standard: 10-15%)
- Weekly: <15% (expected higher due to nature)
- Yearly: <3% (committed users)

## Advertising Strategy (Free Tier)

### Ad Format

**Mid-Roll Video Ads:**
- Length: 15 seconds
- Frequency: Every 3 episodes
- Skippable after 5 seconds
- Reward: "Watch full ad, unlock 1 bonus episode"

**Banner Ads:**
- Home screen bottom
- Non-intrusive
- Dismissable
- Frequency capped (1 per session)

### Ad Experience

**Rules:**
- Never interrupt mid-episode
- Only between episodes
- Max 3 ads per session for free users
- High-quality, relevant ads only
- No malware, misleading, or inappropriate ads

**Ad Quality:**
- Curated advertisers only
- Relevant to demographic (18-35)
- Entertainment, lifestyle, tech brands
- Native ad format (matches app aesthetic)

### Ad Revenue

**CPM Model:** $10-25 CPM (high quality demographic)

**Calculation:**
```
Free users: 60% of 100,000 = 60,000
Daily active (40%): 24,000 users
Avg sessions/day: 2
Episodes per session: 9
Ad breaks per session: 3

Daily ad impressions: 24,000 × 2 × 3 = 144,000
Monthly ad impressions: 144,000 × 30 = 4,320,000
Monthly ad revenue (at $15 CPM): $64,800
```

**Annual Ad Revenue (Year 1):** ~$800,000

### Ad-Free as Premium Value

**Messaging:**
- "Skip the wait, upgrade to Premium"
- "Join 50,000 ad-free viewers"
- "Your time is valuable. Watch uninterrupted."

## Monetization Ethics

### Fair Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Clear, transparent pricing
- Easy cancellation
- Prorated refunds (if applicable)
- No hidden fees
- Honest free tier limitations
- Privacy-first approach

❌ **DON'T:**
- Dark patterns (hard to cancel)
- Automatic renewals without notice
- Selling user data
- Predatory pricing
- Excessive ads for free users
- Bait-and-switch tactics

### Accessibility

- Student discount (40% off with verification)
- Low-income tier (future, partnership with govt programs)
- Accessibility features free for all tiers
- Caption support (all tiers)
